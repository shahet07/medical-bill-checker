## Why ask for an itemized bill
A summary bill shows only a total. An itemized bill lists each service with its date, code, description, units, and charge. It is the only way to check for duplicate charges, services you did not receive, or charges that do not match your insurance EOB.

## How to ask
You can call the billing office or send a written request. Ask for every service listed separately with dates, procedure or revenue codes, units, and charges, plus all payments and adjustments. Ask them to put the account on hold while you wait. Write down the date, the name of the person you spoke with, and any reference number.

## Your right to your records
Under HIPAA, you generally have the right to see and get a copy of your health information, which includes billing records held by your provider or health plan. Covered providers and plans usually must respond within 30 days, with one possible 30-day extension, and may charge only a reasonable, cost-based fee for copies. Many providers give itemized bills for free when asked.
