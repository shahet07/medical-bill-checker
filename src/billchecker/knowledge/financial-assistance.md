## Who offers it
Many hospitals offer free or discounted care based on income, often called charity care or financial assistance. Nonprofit hospitals in the United States are required by federal tax law to have a written financial assistance policy, to publicize it, and to provide a plain-language summary and an application. Many states have their own charity care rules too.

## What nonprofit hospitals must do
A nonprofit hospital must limit what it charges patients who qualify for assistance to no more than the amounts generally billed to insured patients for emergency and other medically necessary care. It must also make reasonable efforts to find out whether you qualify before taking extraordinary collection actions, such as reporting the debt to a credit bureau or suing.

## How to apply
Ask the billing office for the financial assistance policy and application, or look on the hospital's website. You can apply even if you have insurance and even after you have received a bill. You will usually need proof of income and household size. Ask them to put the account on hold while your application is reviewed.
