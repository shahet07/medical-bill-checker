## Errors that are worth checking
Common problems include the same service charged twice, charges for services that were canceled or not received, the wrong number of units, a code that does not match the service you had, insurance payments that were not applied, and the wrong insurance information on file so the claim was denied or never sent.

## How to check
Compare the itemized bill with your own records of the visit and with your insurance EOB. Look for the same code and date appearing more than once, services on the bill that are missing from the EOB, and an amount due higher than what the EOB says you owe.

## What to do if something looks wrong
Contact the billing office, explain what you found, and ask them to review it and send a corrected bill or a written explanation. Ask them to put the account on hold during the review. Keep notes of every call and copies of letters. Flags from this tool are questions to ask, not proof of an error.
