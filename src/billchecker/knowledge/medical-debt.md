## Before a bill goes to collections
If you are questioning a bill, appealing a denial, or applying for financial assistance, tell the provider in writing and ask them to hold the account. Nonprofit hospitals must make reasonable efforts to check whether you qualify for financial assistance before certain collection actions.

## Credit reports
The three nationwide credit bureaus have said that paid medical collection debt is removed from credit reports, that medical collection debt is not reported until it has been unpaid for a year, and that medical collections under $500 are not reported. Rules about medical debt and credit reports have been changing, so check the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau for the latest information.

## If a debt collector contacts you
You can ask the collector to validate the debt, meaning send details about what it is and who it is owed to. Check it against your bills and EOBs before paying.
