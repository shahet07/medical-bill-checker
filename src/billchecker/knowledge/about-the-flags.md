## Flags are questions, not errors
Each flag is something worth asking the billing office about. A flag is not proof that the bill is wrong. The evidence shown with each flag is copied from your bill or EOB so you can check it yourself.

## Balance and payment checks
"Amount due doesn't match your insurance statement" means the bill asks for more or less than the EOB says you owe. "Insurance payment may not be applied" means the EOB shows your insurer paid more than the bill shows in payments and adjustments.

## Line checks
"Possible duplicate charge" means the same code, date, units, and charge appear more than once. "Billed for a service your insurer denied" means a service is on the bill that the EOB shows as denied or not processed. "Service not on your insurance statement" means a service on the bill is missing from the EOB for that date and may not have been sent to insurance. "Charge differs from what your insurer was billed" means the bill and the EOB show different charges for the same service.

## Other checks
"Out-of-network billing for in-network care" means the bill says out of network but the EOB processed the claim as in network. "No itemized list of charges" means the bill shows only a total.
