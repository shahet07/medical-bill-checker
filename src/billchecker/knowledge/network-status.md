## In network and out of network
In-network providers have a contract with your insurer and agree to accept the allowed amount. Out-of-network providers do not have a contract, so your plan may pay less or nothing, and you may owe more.

## Balance billing
Balance billing is when an out-of-network provider bills you for the difference between what they charged and what your plan allowed. In-network providers are not allowed to balance bill you for covered services. The No Surprises Act also bans balance billing in many emergency and in-network-facility situations.

## If your bill says out of network
If the bill says the provider is out of network but your EOB processed the claim as in network, ask both the provider and the insurer to confirm the network status. Check your insurer's provider directory and keep a copy of what it showed when you got care.
