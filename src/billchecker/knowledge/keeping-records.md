title: Keeping records and following up
sources: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau | https://www.consumerfinance.gov/
---
## What to keep
Keep every bill, EOB, receipt, letter, and payment record for each visit. Write down notes from each phone call: the date, the name of the person, what they said, and any reference or ticket number.

## Following up
Send important requests in writing and keep a copy. If you mail a letter, consider a method with proof of delivery. If you do not hear back in a few weeks, call again and refer to your earlier contact. Ask the provider to put the account on hold while a question, appeal, or assistance application is open, so it is not sent to collections.

## Deadlines to watch
Insurance appeals, good faith estimate disputes, and financial assistance applications can have deadlines, so act soon after you notice a problem.
