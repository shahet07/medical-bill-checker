## Before you pay
Make sure the bill is correct first: check it against your EOB and ask for an itemized bill if needed. Paying a bill does not always stop you from questioning it later, but it is easier to fix errors before you pay.

## Asking for a plan or a discount
Many providers offer interest-free payment plans if you ask. Say what monthly amount you can afford. Some providers also offer a discount for paying in full or for patients paying on their own. Ask whether you qualify for financial assistance at the same time.

## Get it in writing
Ask for the payment plan terms in writing, including the monthly amount, the number of payments, and whether any interest or fees apply. Ask the provider to keep the account out of collections while you make payments as agreed. Be careful about paying with a credit card or medical credit card that charges interest, because that can cost more than a provider's own plan.
